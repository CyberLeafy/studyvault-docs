# Frequently Asked Questions


<details>
  <summary>Is StudyVault free to use?</summary>

  Yes. StudyVault is completely free to use.

</details> 



<details>
  <summary>Does StudyVault require an internet connection?</summary>

  No. StudyVault is an offline-first application. Once installed, all features work without an internet connection.

</details>



<details>
<summary>
Where are my screenshots stored?
</summary>

All screenshots are stored locally on your device. StudyVault does not upload your images to any external server.

</details>


<details>
<summary>
  Can I import multiple screenshots at once?
</summary>

Yes. You can select multiple images from any supported app and import them into StudyVault in a single operation.

</details>


<details>
<summary>
How do I import screenshots from another app?
</summary>

1. Select one or more images.
2. Tap **Share**.
3. Choose **StudyVault**.
4. Select a Subject, Chapter, and Section.

Your screenshots will be imported automatically.

</details>


<details>
<summary>
    What happens if a chapter or section does not exist?
</summary>

Create the required chapter or section first, then continue the import process.

</details>


<details>
  <summary>
    Can I edit or delete the default subjects?
  </summary>

No. The default subjects (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Mathematics) are built into the application and cannot be edited or deleted.

</details>


<details>
  <summary>Can I create my own chapters?</summary>

Yes. You can create custom chapters and organize your study material as needed.

</details>

<details>
  <summary>Can I rename chapters and sections?</summary>

Yes. Chapters and sections can be renamed at any time.

</details>

<details>
  <summary>Can I delete chapters or sections?</summary>

No. Chapters and sections cannot be deleted. This helps preserve your study structure and prevents accidental data loss.

</details>

<details>
  <summary>How do I delete a screenshot?</summary>

Long press the screenshot, confirm the deletion, and the image will be permanently removed from the selected section.

</details>

<details>
  <summary>Can I zoom in on screenshots?</summary>

Yes. The built-in image viewer supports pinch-to-zoom and swipe gestures for navigating between screenshots.

</details>

<details>
  <summary>Can I use StudyVault without creating an account?</summary>

Yes. StudyVault does not require an account or sign-in.

</details>

<details>
  <summary>Is my data private?</summary>

Yes. Your screenshots and study data remain on your device. StudyVault does not collect or upload your personal data.

</details>
