# Import Your First Screenshot

StudyVault allows you to import screenshots directly from the Android Share menu.

### Step 1: Open Your Screenshot

Open any screenshot from your Gallery or another supported app.

### Step 2: Share the Image

Tap the **Share** button and select **StudyVault** from the list of available apps.

### Step 3: Choose Where to Save

Select:

- Subject
- Chapter
- Section

:::warning
If they don't exist yet, create them before saving.
:::

### Step 4: Save

Tap **Save**.

Your screenshot will be stored locally and organized under the selected section.

### View Your Screenshot

Open the selected section to view the imported screenshot.


:::tip
Importing screenshots through the Share menu is the fastest way to organize your study material.
:::
