# Chapters

Chapters help you divide a subject into smaller study units.

Every section and screenshot belongs to a chapter.

## Create a Chapter

1. Open a subject.
2. Tap the **Add** button.
3. Enter the chapter name.
4. Tap **Save**.

The new chapter will appear in the chapter list.

## Rename a Chapter

1. Long press the section.
2. Open **Rename** box.
3. Rename existing **chapter** name.
4. Save your changes.

## Deleting Chapters

:::important

Chapters **cannot be deleted**.

This design helps preserve the structure of your study material and prevents accidental data loss.

:::

:::tip

Use clear and descriptive chapter names, such as:

- Motion
- Thermodynamics
- Electrostatics
- Organic Chemistry

This makes your screenshots easier to find later.

:::
