# Planner Guide

The **Planner** helps you plan and manage your tests in one place. It makes it easier to organize upcoming tests, keep track of your schedule, and quickly access your planned tests.

## Getting Started

Follow these steps to get started with Planner.

### 1. Open Planner

Navigate to the **Planner** section in your application to view and manage your planned tests.

### 2. Organize Upcoming Tests

Use Planner to organize your upcoming tests so you can easily see which tests are scheduled next.

### 3. Track Your Test Schedule

Keep track of your test dates and schedule to stay organized and prepare in advance.

### 4. Access Planned Tests

Open Planner whenever you need to review your upcoming tests and check your schedule.

## Benefits of Using Planner

- **Better organization:** Keep your upcoming tests organized.
- **Schedule tracking:** Stay aware of your planned tests and important dates.
- **Quick access:** Find your planned tests easily whenever you need them.

## Tips for Effective Planning

- Review your upcoming tests regularly.
- Keep your test schedule up to date.
- Plan your preparation time around your scheduled tests.
