# Study Tracker

Study Tracker helps you monitor your preparation by tracking the duration of your study plan.

## Create a Tracker

1. Click the **Set Tracker Button** on home page.
2. Select a **Start Date**.
3. Select an **End Date**.
4. Save the tracker.

Only one tracker can be active at a time.

## Daily Progress

The tracker automatically calculates your progress based on the selected start and end dates.

Each day is marked as time passes, allowing you to see your preparation timeline at a glance.

## Update Your Tracker

You can update the start date or end date whenever your study plan changes.

:::tip
- Choose realistic start and end dates.
- Keep your tracker updated if your exam schedule changes.
- Check your tracker regularly to stay motivated.
:::
