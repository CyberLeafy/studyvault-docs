# Subjects

Subjects are the top level of organization in StudyVault.

Every chapter, section, and screenshot belongs to a subject.

## Default Subjects

StudyVault comes with four built-in subjects:

- Physics
- Chemistry
- Biology
- Mathematics

These subjects are available immediately after installing the app.

:::note[Protected Subjects]

The default subjects are built into the application and **cannot be edited or deleted**. This ensures a consistent structure for organizing your study material.

:::
