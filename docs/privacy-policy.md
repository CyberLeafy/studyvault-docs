# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 2026_

StudyVault is designed with privacy as a priority. The application works offline and keeps your study material on your device.

## Data Storage

StudyVault stores all application data locally on your device, including:

- Subjects
- Chapters
- Sections
- Study Tracker
- Imported screenshots

Your data is **never uploaded** to our servers.

## Internet Connection

StudyVault does not require an internet connection for its core features.

You can organize, view, and manage your study material completely offline.

## Permissions

StudyVault may request the following permissions:

### Photos and Media

Required to:

- Import screenshots into the app.
- Save and manage screenshots.

These files remain on your device.

## Personal Information

StudyVault does **not** collect personal information such as:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Location
- Contacts

No account or sign-in is required.

## Third-Party Services

StudyVault does not use cloud storage to store your screenshots.

If future versions introduce optional online services, this policy will be updated accordingly.

## Data Deletion

You have full control over your data.

You can:

- Delete screenshots from the gallery.
- Remove the application to delete all locally stored data.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when new features are introduced.

The latest version will always be available in the StudyVault documentation.

## Contact

If you have questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the project maintainer through the official project repository.
